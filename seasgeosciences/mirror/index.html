MERIDIAN GEOSCIENCE

Clarity beneath the surface

The most effective way to control project safety, cost, and schedule begins with fully characterizing foundation soil uncertainties and developing a thorough understanding of ground stratigraphy and engineering properties. At Meridian, our multidisciplinary team of soil physicians combines advanced, integrated research and geoscience exploration techniques to generate engineering insights that enable safe, cost‑effective, and efficient project delivery.

Sound soil engineering parameters come from advanced site characterization techniques and deep expertise

Meridian works on high-stakes offshore developments where data quality and traceability are critical. We assess ground conditions offshore, review investigation methods in real time, and maintain a transparent workflow from data acquisition through to interpretation. The result is reliable information that project teams can confidently rely on as the work progresses.
